# Design (current state — descriptive)

AstraZeneca's newsroom: a white, document-like page. Lexia (Adobe Fonts, licensed) carries headings,
body and buttons; Helvetica Neue appears in utility text. Colour is restrained: dark grey text
(#3c4242), mulberry (#830051) for results-page accents and table headers, purple (#3c1053) accents,
light grey surfaces (#ebefee). Cards round at 8px. Icons come from the site's own icon font
(az-webfont). Two templates were captured: a text press release (date, standfirst h3s, body,
references, sidebar) and a results release (hero image, in-page tab strip, downloads, KPI cards,
financial table, therapy-area cards, pipeline stat cards).
